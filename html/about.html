About me

Hardeep Patel.
Engineer. Future founder.

Based in San Francisco. Currently a founding software engineer at Context Labs, working across product, backend systems, and production AI.

My background

I like being close to
what I’m building.

At Context Labs, that means working directly with enterprise teams, understanding how they work, and taking a feature from the first conversation through deployment.

Before that, I worked on agricultural software at Toya, payment systems at Infibeam, flood forecasting at ISRO, and graph algorithms at IIT Roorkee. Those experiences gave me different ways to approach the same question: what needs to improve, and how will we know it did?

How I want to work

A fast-moving team.
Room to take ownership.

I’m drawn to teams where engineers stay close to customers, make thoughtful decisions quickly, and take responsibility for what they ship. I enjoy working across the interface, the service, and the deployment when that’s what the problem needs.

I want to keep learning from people who set a high bar for engineering and product judgment. For me, moving quickly also means making the work understandable, measuring the result, and improving it once people start using it.

Looking ahead

One day, build
a company of my own.

My long-term goal is to become a founder. I want to build a product around a problem I understand deeply, earn the trust of its users, and put together a team that cares about the details.

Right now, I’m focused on becoming the engineer that ambition requires: stronger at systems, closer to customers, and better at turning an uncertain problem into something useful.
